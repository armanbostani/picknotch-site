The Velotric Fold 1 solves the usual folding-bike compromise on the ride side: it keeps a full 750W motor, fat 20 x 3in tyres and a 608Wh battery, so it rides like a normal fat-tyre e-bike rather than a twitchy, underpowered toy. Folded, it shrinks to 38 x 19 x 33in — small enough for a car boot, an RV bay or a cupboard. This is a research-based verdict built on the spec sheet and owner feedback. The honest catch is weight: at around 67lb it folds small but stays heavy, so it's the bike you fold to store or transport, not one to carry up several flights of stairs.
