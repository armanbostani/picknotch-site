The TST GT63 is a compact electric dirt bike that solves a real problem: most e-motos are too big, too fast and too unaccountable for the teens who want them most. With a 28.7in seat height fitting riders from 3'11" to 6'1", a key ignition, three speed modes and hydraulic brakes at both ends, it's built to be someone's first powered two-wheeler — and at $1,199 it undercuts almost everything comparable. Our reservation is transparency: the 45mph and 210Nm claims outrun what owners actually report (mid-30s stock), and TST doesn't publish the bike's weight.
