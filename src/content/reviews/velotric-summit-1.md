The Velotric Summit 1 is one of the few sub-$2,000 e-bikes that earns the "electric mountain bike" label rather than borrowing it. A torque sensor pairs your effort with a 750W motor and 90Nm of torque, a 120mm air-sprung fork with lockout keeps the front end planted, and a 705.6Wh battery is good for a claimed 70 miles. This is a research-based verdict built on the spec sheet and owner feedback. The honest catch is the chassis: it's a hardtail, so there's no rear suspension for fast, rocky descents, and it's a heavy eMTB. As a climber and trail explorer at $1,999 it's superb — just remember it goes up better than it comes down.
