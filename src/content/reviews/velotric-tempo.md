The Velotric Tempo is the answer to a complaint about most e-bikes: that they're heavy, bulky and obviously electric. At a claimed 39lb it rides, lifts and parks like a normal bike, with a clean cable-integrated frame, hydraulic brakes and a surprising amount of clever tech — SensorSwap assist, cruise control, over-the-air updates and a heart-rate Pulse Mode. This is a research-based verdict built on the spec sheet and owner feedback. The honest catch is that the lightness comes from restraint: a 350W motor and a 374Wh battery are plenty for flat-city commuting, but this is a bike that feels like a bike, not a hill-flattening, cargo-hauling workhorse.
