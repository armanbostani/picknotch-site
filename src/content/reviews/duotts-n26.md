The Duotts N26 is one of the more unusual bikes at its price: a dual-motor, all-wheel-drive fat-tyre e-bike with full suspension, a 960Wh Samsung-cell battery and big 203mm hydraulic brakes — and it ships to the UK for €1,399. Front and rear hub motors give it real traction on loose ground, and the dual-crown fork plus rear shock make it genuinely comfortable. This is a research-based verdict built on the spec sheet and the brand's own figures. The honest catch comes in two parts: at 39kg (86lb) it's a heavy machine to lift, store or recover, and — like the C29 — its full-power 1,500W setup isn't road-legal as a bicycle in the UK or EU, where the limit is 250W and 15.5mph. As an off-road, private-land AWD plaything it's a lot of fun for the money; just buy it with both of those in mind.
