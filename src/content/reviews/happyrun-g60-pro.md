The HappyRun G60 Pro is the sensible counterpoint to the brand's wilder machines: a moped-style fat-tyre cruiser that leads with comfort, range and — unusually at $1,099 — full UL 2849 certification. A 48V 25Ah (1,200Wh) battery backs a claimed 70+ miles, full suspension and 20 x 4in tyres soak up the road, and a half-twist throttle plus a 7-speed drivetrain cover both lazy cruising and range-stretching. This is a research-based verdict built on the spec sheet and the brand's own figures. The honest catch is the physics: at 93lb it's heavy to lift or store above ground, and the 20mph top speed makes it a relaxed cruiser rather than a quick one. For a comfortable, certified, big-range moped at this price, that's a fair trade.
