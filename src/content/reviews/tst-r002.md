The TST R002 is the cheapest genuinely complete moped-style e-bike we've reviewed: $799 buys a 1500W-peak hub motor with 90Nm, full suspension, 20×4in fat tires, an app that unlocks 32mph, and a road-ready kit of lights, turn signals, rack and fenders. Independent reviewers landed where the 377 on-site owner reviews (4.79★ average) did — it's a blast for the money. The honest catch is component grade: mechanical disc brakes on a bike this quick, a bench seat that turns hard after 40 minutes, and some early-mile squeaks.
