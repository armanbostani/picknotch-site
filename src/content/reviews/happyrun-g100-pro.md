The HappyRun G100 Pro is a power-per-dollar argument with two batteries strapped to it: a 72V system, 6000W of peak power, a claimed 50mph top speed and up to 78 miles of range — for $2,199, around $1,100 under its regular price. It's full-suspension, fat-tyred and dressed like a scrambler. This is a research-based verdict built on the spec sheet and the brand's own figures. The honest catch is twofold: at a claimed 50mph it's an off-road or private-land machine rather than a road-legal bicycle, and at 159lb it's a genuine motorcycle-grade lift to move, store or recover. Buy it for the thrills and the space to use them — not as an everyday commuter.
