The TST Carrier 20 is a genuine long-tail cargo bike at a price that usually buys a plain commuter: a 450lb total payload, an extended passenger-ready rear deck, a low step-through frame and a 1300W motor to move it all — for $1,099. This is a research-based verdict built on the spec sheet and the brand's own figures. The honest catch is the physics and the brand: at 90lb before you load anything, it's heavy to park, push and store, and TST is a young direct-to-consumer brand, so long-term parts and warranty support are unproven. But if you'll actually use the capacity — school runs, shopping, a passenger — it does the job of a much pricier cargo bike, and replaces real car trips doing it.
