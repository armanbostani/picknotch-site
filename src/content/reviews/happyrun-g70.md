The HappyRun G70 is a moped-style cargo hauler that gets the expensive things right: a 1,824Wh dual-battery system with reviewer-verified 45–50 mile throttle range, hydraulic brakes, a passenger-ready bench and a lighting package straight off a small motorcycle — for $1,599. The honest trade-off is everything around the edges: 98lb of steel, laggy cadence assist, clunky shifting and a brand whose after-sales support record is mixed. Stock also comes and goes — at the time of writing both colours showed sold out, so check the link.
