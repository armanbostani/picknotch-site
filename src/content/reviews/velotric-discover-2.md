The Velotric Discover 2 is the value answer to the Discover 3: it keeps the parts that matter — a 750W motor, a 706Wh LG-cell battery with up to 75 miles of claimed range, and full UL 2849 certification — and sells for $1,699, $300 less than its newer sibling. This is a research-based verdict built on the spec sheet and owner feedback. The honest catch is comfort: the Discover 2 runs a basic 80mm hydraulic suspension fork instead of the Discover 3's air fork and suspension seatpost. On flat commutes you'll never miss them; on rough roads, that comfort hardware is precisely what the extra $300 buys.
