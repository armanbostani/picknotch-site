The TST Defender 26 is a spec-for-money play, and a loud one: a 1300W motor with 90Nm, dual hydraulic brakes, a 720Wh battery good for a claimed 65 miles, and a free rear rack and fender set — all for $999, roughly half its $2,077 list price. This is a research-based verdict built on the spec sheet and the brand's own figures. The honest catch is the usual budget trade: TST is a young direct-to-consumer brand, so parts and warranty support are unproven, and the Defender is a hardtail (front suspension only) on a fairly heavy 72lb frame. If you want the most fat-tyre power and kit your money can buy and you're comfortable with a newer brand, it's hard to argue with.
