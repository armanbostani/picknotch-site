The Duotts C29 is a value play, and on paper it's a strong one: a 29in trekking e-bike with a 21-speed drivetrain, a suspension fork, hydraulic-or-mechanical disc brakes, a rear rack, mudguards and lights — from €819, and shipping to the UK. A removable 48V 15Ah battery claims up to 90km of assisted range, with a dual-battery option for riders who want more. This is a research-based verdict built on the spec sheet and the brand's own figures. The honest catch comes in two parts: Duotts is a young budget brand, so long-term parts and warranty support are unproven; and the headline 750W/52km/h "International version" isn't road-legal as a bicycle in the UK or EU, where the legal limit is 250W and 15.5mph. Buy it for the kit-for-money — with both of those in mind.
