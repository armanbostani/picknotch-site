The HappyRun G300 Pro is the budget answer to the Sur Ron question: a 72V, 6500W-peak electric dirt bike that independent reviewers clocked at 46–48mph on GPS, with adjustable inverted forks and moto-sized hydraulic brakes, for $1,959 in the linked Black trim ($1,899 in Orange or Blue). Electrek's verdict captured it well — a mix of quality parts and merely acceptable ones, nothing dangerous, hugely fun. The honest trade-offs: it weighs 136.8lb, it belongs on private land rather than public roads, and HappyRun's after-sales record is the real gamble.
